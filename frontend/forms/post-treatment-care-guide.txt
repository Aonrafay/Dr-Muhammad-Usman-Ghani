General Care (First 24 Hours)
- Avoid very hot beverages and hard foods.
- Do not chew on the treated side unless instructed.
- Keep the area clean but gentle while brushing.

Pain Management
- Take prescribed medication exactly as directed.
- Use a cold compress externally for swelling in short intervals.
- Do not self-medicate beyond advised dosage.

Oral Hygiene
- Continue brushing with a soft toothbrush.
- Rinse with lukewarm salt water if advised.
- Avoid aggressive rinsing or touching treated area.

When to Contact the Clinic Immediately
- Persistent heavy bleeding
- Swelling that worsens after 24 hours
- Fever or signs of infection
- Severe pain not controlled by prescribed medication
