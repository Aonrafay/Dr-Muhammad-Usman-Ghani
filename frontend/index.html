Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule a dental checkup?

Most patients benefit from a checkup every six months, though shorter intervals may be advised based on gum or decay risk.

Do you treat children, Dr. Ghani?

Yes, pediatric appointments are available with prevention-focused counseling for parents.

What if I have dental anxiety?

Please inform us in advance. We use calm communication and comfort-first treatment pacing.

How should I request emergency care?
