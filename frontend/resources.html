Resources for Better Daily Oral Care

Learn practical routines, review common questions, and access pre-visit and aftercare forms.

Brushing Technique

Use a soft brush at a 45-degree angle and clean all tooth surfaces for two full minutes.

Flossing Habit

Floss once daily and curve around each tooth to remove plaque from gumline contact points.

Diet and Enamel

Reduce sugary snacks between meals and rinse with water after acidic drinks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule dental checkups?

Most patients benefit from a checkup every six months, but Dr. Ghani may recommend shorter intervals based on gum or decay risk.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We provide pediatric-friendly appointments focused on prevention, comfort, and habit coaching for parents.

What if I have dental anxiety?

Please tell us before your visit. We use calm communication, predictable treatment pacing, and comfort-first techniques.

Can I book an emergency slot online?

For emergencies, call our hotline directly so we can prioritize pain or trauma cases immediately.

Aftercare Checklist

  • A

    First 24 Hours

    Avoid very hot drinks and hard chewing on treated side.

  • B

    Pain Control

    Take prescribed medication only as advised by the dentist.

  • C

    Follow-up

    Report prolonged pain, swelling, or bleeding to the clinic immediately.
